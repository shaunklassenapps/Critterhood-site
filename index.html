Know when wildlife is moving through your neighbourhood

Critterhood is a community-driven wildlife alert app. Report sightings and get notified when wildlife is spotted near the places that matter to you.

Free on iPhone and Android.

Features

Everything you need to stay aware

Report a Sighting

Report it in seconds. A few taps puts it on the map, and others watching the area get a heads-up right away.

Sightings Map

See what's been reported, filter by species or time window, and tap any pin for the full details.

Alert Zones

Watch the places that matter the most to you — home, school, a trail, or anywhere else. Get an alert as soon as wildlife is reported in any of them.

Community Driven

Share any sighting with anyone, even if they don't have the app. Flag reports that look off or animals that have moved on.

Wildlife Info

Know what to do if you meet a bear, cougar, coyote, and more. Practical tips for 11 species, plus links to official wildlife agencies.

Anonymous

No account, no sign-in, no personal profile. Sightings you submit are never linked to your identity.

Wildlife-friendly reporting

Critterhood never passes sightings on to any agency or authority. It's simply about awareness, so people can give wildlife space and take action if needed to help keep everyone safe, including the animals.

If an animal is a threat to anyone's safety, contact 911 or your local wildlife authority directly.

How it works

From sighting to alert in three steps

Someone spots wildlife

A Critterhood user submits a quick report of what they saw.

The sighting is checked against your zones

Critterhood looks at whether it's inside one of your alert zones, during the hours you've chosen.

You get notified

An alert pops up on your phone right away, letting you know about the visitor.

Alert zones and time windows

Alerts on your terms

Critterhood keeps watch so you don't have to keep checking the map. You decide where, when, and how often you hear about it.

  • 📍Where — set alert zones around home, school, or anywhere else, and size each one to fit.
  • 🗓️When — choose the days and hours you want alerts.
  • 🔕How often — hold back repeat alerts for the same animal in the same zone.
